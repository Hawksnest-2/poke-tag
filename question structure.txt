to understand the desired question philosophy and framework refer to the following:

Core Philosophies:

 - quiz questions are intended to be a fun experience, not a test or exam... by having fun, success, but also a challenge the players may accidentally learn something as a side effect.
 - Expert questions should measure understanding and familiarity with a field, not memorization of facts.

Avoid questions that are primarily:
 - Who won...
 - What year...
 - Who played...
 - What port...
 - What RFC...
 - What actor...

Preferred types of questons would be:
 - Concepts
 - Techniques
 - Systems
 - Terminology
 - Processes
 - Strategies
 - Relationships

additional difficulty definitions:
Easy
 - Typical Audience
     - Ages 8+
     - General public
     - Casual players
 - Goal
     - A player should feel:
       - "I know this."
       - A casual person should know most of these.
       - 50-80% correct

   Examples:
     DNA
     Mount Everest
     Basketball
     Mona Lisa
     Pikachu

   Think:
     - General cultural literacy.


Medium
 - Typical Audience
     - Ages 12+
     - Middle School
     - High School
     - Interested adults
 - Goal
     - A player should feel:
       - "I've learned this somewhere."
       - Someone who paid attention in school or enjoys the topic should do well.
       - Expected Success
          - 40-70%

   Examples:
     Photosynthesis
     Renaissance
     Penalty Kick
     Photoshop Layer

   Think:
   - Interested hobbyist.


Hard
 - Typical Audience
     - Ages 15+
     - High School Honors
     - College Freshman
     - Dedicated hobbyists
 - Goal
     - A player should feel:
       - "I recognize that term."
     - Requires meaningful exposure to the subject.
     - Expected Success
     - 15-40%

   Examples:
     Ecotone
     Libero
     Constructivism
     Showrunner
     Big O Notation

   Think:
   - Enthusiast level.


Expert
 - Typical Audience
   - 18+
   - College educated
   - Serious enthusiasts
   - Practitioners
   - Professionals
 - Goal
   - Requires subject-specific knowledge.
   - A player should feel:
     - "I know this because I'm into the subject."
      Not:
     - "I memorized trivia."
   - Expected Success
     - 5-20%

   Examples:
     Ekphrasis
     VO2 Max
     CRISPR
     Ludonarrative Dissonance
     Thermohaline Circulation

   Think:
   - Practitioner, student, coach, or serious hobbyist.
   - Build Expert categories that reward understanding of a field rather than memorization of trivia.

Question Writing Rules
 - We gradually settled on these:
    - Prefer Context

  Bad:
   - What is a Metroidvania?

  Better:
   - A game genre featuring interconnected exploration and ability-gated progression is commonly called a...
   - Avoid Definition Flashcards

  Bad:
   - Define hamartia.

  Better:
   - The tragic flaw contributing to a hero's downfall is known as...

  Prefer Recognition Over Recall
   - Good trivia lets the player think:
      - I've heard that before...
     instead of:
      - I need to memorize obscure facts.

  Concept > Date
   - If choosing between:
      - What year was...
     and
      - What concept explains...
   - we consistently chose the concept.

   - try to avoid providing the answer directly in the question, especially for Hard and Expert difficulties, . 
     Example: If the answer is "homing pigeon"... the words 'homing' or 'pigeon' should not be in the question.
